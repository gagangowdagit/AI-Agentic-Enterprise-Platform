Customer Insights Portal

Objective
This initiative creates a single executive experience for customer growth KPIs, retention trends, and product adoption metrics across the business.

Key themes
- Revenue and retention signals
- Product usage and activation metrics
- Executive-ready operational dashboards

Go-live notes
Leadership requested that the first release remain focused on the most significant KPI set. It should avoid overloading the dashboard with lower-value operational noise.

Dependencies
- Data freshness checks
- Executive metric alignment review
- Pipeline QA validation before beta cutover

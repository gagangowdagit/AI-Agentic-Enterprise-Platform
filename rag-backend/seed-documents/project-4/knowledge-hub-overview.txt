Enterprise Knowledge Hub

Objective
The Knowledge Hub consolidates product documentation, onboarding guides, and operational playbooks into a searchable enterprise workspace.

Deliverables
- Reusable onboarding templates
- Central operational playbook library
- Department-specific archive and search experience

Key principle
Knowledge should be easy to locate and simple to update when processes evolve.

Current phase
The project is in final archive cleanup and governance review before full adoption by all business units.

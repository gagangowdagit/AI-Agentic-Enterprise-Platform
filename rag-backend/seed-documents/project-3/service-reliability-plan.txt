Service Reliability Program

Purpose
The Service Reliability Program is designed to improve observability, alert quality, and service ownership across production teams.

Primary goals
- Stabilize SLO tracking and governance
- Improve alert routing to reduce noisy incident traffic
- Ensure cross-functional operational readiness for future launches

Pillar areas
- SLO governance
- Incident rehearsal
- Ownership mapping and service catalog updates

Summary
Operational findings from the most recent incident drill point to a need for clearer alert ownership and better playbook consistency during high-traffic periods.

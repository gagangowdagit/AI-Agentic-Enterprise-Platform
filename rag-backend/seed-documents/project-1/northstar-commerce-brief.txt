Northstar Commerce Upgrade

Overview
The Northstar Commerce Upgrade program is focused on modernizing the checkout experience and stabilizing pricing logic before peak-season traffic. Current priorities include payment gateway failover, inventory synchronization, and a smoother customer pricing flow.

Scope
- Improve checkout resilience during gateway outage events
- Reduce pricing latency under high-volume traffic
- Validate rollback and support runbooks for release windows

Dependencies
- Pricing service migration
- Customer support notifications
- Payment orchestration validation

Risk notes
- Time-of-day traffic spikes remain the largest operational risk
- Edge-case failover logic needs explicit QA validation before final approval

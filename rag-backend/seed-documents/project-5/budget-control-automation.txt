Budget Control Automation

Scope
The automation initiative is designed to improve approval routing, forecast variance visibility, and operating expense discipline.

Objectives
- Route spend exceptions to the correct stakeholders
- Surface variance alerts before budget overruns occur
- Standardize downstream reporting for monthly close cycles

Approach
Finance and operations will jointly review all exception pathways and ensure approval thresholds are tied to local operating plans.

Success metric
Effective control should reduce time-to-decision while improving budget accountability across teams.

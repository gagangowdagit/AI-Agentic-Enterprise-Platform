Shiprocket IPO — Price Data Summary

Important correction: The earlier statement that the listing price was
₹163 was incorrect.

Verified figures: • Pre-IPO/private transaction price: ₹163.14 per share
(reported in Shiprocket’s IPO disclosure for certain pre-IPO
transactions, adjusted for the later bonus issue). • IPO price band:
₹92–₹97 per share. • Final IPO issue price: ₹97 per share. • Listing
date: 19 August 2026. • NSE listing/opening price: ₹131 per share. • BSE
listing/opening price: ₹129 per share.

Therefore: Pre-IPO/private transaction price = ₹163.14 IPO issue price =
₹97 NSE listing price = ₹131 BSE listing price = ₹129

Note: ₹163.14 and ₹163 should NOT be treated as the Shiprocket listing
price. ₹163.14 refers to disclosed private/pre-IPO transactions, while
the actual stock-market debut was ₹131 on NSE and ₹129 on BSE.

Sources: • NSE listing circular: Issue price ₹97. • Business Standard,
19 Aug 2026: NSE debut ₹131 and BSE debut ₹129. • Shiprocket IPO
disclosure: ₹163.14 pre-IPO transaction price.
